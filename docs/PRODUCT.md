# Here — product spec (MVP)

## What it is

A quiet private window into the everyday life of someone important to you.

It is **not** messaging for couples, a WhatsApp replacement, a tracker, or a relationship coach.

## Job to be done

When someone important lives far away, help both of you keep feeling part of ordinary life without being responsible for a conversation.

## Core surfaces

### Today

Each person has **one** daily canvas per their **local calendar day** (timezone on their profile). Optional:

- manual presence state
- mood
- short thought (≤ 180 characters)
- one photo
- one song

They may edit throughout the day. The other person sees the latest version. No reply is required.

### Voice drops

Asynchronous, max 30 seconds. Not a chat thread. Multiple per day are allowed. No transcription. No AI.

### Reactions

One active reaction per person per item: Seen, laugh, Here, heart. Tapping the same reaction removes it. **Seen is never automatic.**

### Memories

Existing content, grouped year → month → day, using the **sender’s local date**. Month-by-month navigation. No separate generation job.

### Connection

Exactly **one active connection** per person. Invite link/code, 7-day expiry, single use, revocable. Disconnect hides the other person’s private content; own content remains.

## Language

Use: your person, your connection, your space.

Avoid required romantic labels.

## Out of scope

See `docs/FUTURE.md`. No push notifications, no AI summaries, no streaks, no groups.
